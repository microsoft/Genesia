# Why Genesia

[Back to the homepage highlights](../README.md#why-genesia)

This document explains the product thinking behind Genesia's five homepage highlights.
It provides supporting detail for introductions, presentations and future documentation.

## Accelerate More Than the Build

AI agents are making it faster to turn an idea into working software. Building,
however, is only one part of a product's lifecycle. Teams still need to discover
needs, decide which problems matter, compare concepts, evaluate designs, plan a
launch and understand what to improve as a product evolves. Faster implementation
does not automatically make those decisions faster or better.

Genesia helps address that gap by automating Persona sessions, structured questions
and analysis. Teams can explore an early concept, evaluate a prototype or investigate
a proposed change using the same research workflow. Shorter feedback cycles help
research and decision-making keep pace with implementation across the product
lifecycle. The aim is to accelerate more of the work that moves a product forward,
not only the part that produces code.

This reduces repeated setup and execution work without promising a fixed turnaround
time. Study scope, materials, model performance and researcher review still determine
how long a useful result takes.

## Personas That Experience Before They Answer

For experience-based studies, a Persona brings its supplied background, goals and
preferences into the product context. In a live prototype it can choose what matters
to it, try relevant actions, inspect outcomes and decide when to stop. Different
Personas can take different paths through the same product.

The same logical Persona then answers using its accumulated experience. Research
questions are introduced after experience, rather than acting as a checklist of
things it must discover. Its feedback has both a personal perspective and a record
of what it encountered.

The objective is not to manufacture disagreement. Personas may reach the same
preference for different reasons, leave needs unresolved, or have no preference.
Recorded studies retain the same identity and context while reviewing supplied
evidence, without pretending to operate the recorded application.

## Turn What You Have into a Research Environment

The questions worth investigating often arrive before a complete product exists.
Genesia can use concept documents, interface screenshots, authorized Figma exports
and recorded journeys as research inputs. It assesses the supplied content and
organizes supported observations into context, UI cards or a connected state graph.
When a live prototype is available, Personas can observe and act in an isolated
browser without that compilation step.

This lets research begin with the product materials available at each stage.
A recording supports review of a fixed journey; a live prototype supports choices
and actions. Neither a screenshot nor a generated graph establishes that an
unseen interaction works. The environment keeps those distinctions visible so
the study can stay within what its materials actually support.

## Turn Individual Feedback into Actionable Insights

Teams need more than a collection of answers. They need to understand which needs
recur, where perspectives differ, what tradeoffs matter and what deserves further
investigation. Genesia supports interviews, surveys, design reviews and task-outcome
reviews using the same participant context. Live comparisons let the same Persona
encounter multiple versions before explaining its choices.

Analysis organizes eligible responses into findings and recommendations, retaining
supporting answers, counterpoints and uncertainty. Researchers can check the original
responses and experience evidence instead of accepting an unexplained summary.
Uncommon feedback can matter even when it does not represent the majority.

Saved responses can be analyzed again with a different focus without repeating the
product experience. This creates another interpretation of the same material, not
new observations or an independent replication. Profile-only surveys also support
questions that do not require a product environment; they do not invent experience
evidence to justify their answers.

## Make Research Easier to Start and Reuse

Genesia separates three parts that teams can configure around their research:

| Building block | What it contributes |
|---|---|
| **Persona Agent** | The participant's supplied identity, goals and perspective, carried through its choices and feedback. |
| **Environment** | What the participant can observe or do, the results of those actions, and the evidence available to the study. |
| **Research task** | What the team wants to learn, which methods it uses, and the questions or criteria that guide the research. |

The SDK and CLI connect these parts into an executable workflow with saved
configuration and results. Teams can change a cohort, revise a prototype or ask
different questions without building a separate research platform. They reuse the
workflow, not a promise of identical model outputs or automatically comparable studies.

Individuals and small teams can start with the [100 fictional profiles](../data/personas/global-digital-100/README.md),
the included examples and their own OpenAI-compatible model provider. They can also
bring authorized profiles or generate, review and approve a study-specific cohort.
Live studies still require an isolated prototype, and all studies need deliberate
questions and data permissions.

Reuse and configurable execution budgets help teams manage setup effort and model
usage. Model charges and human review still apply; lower setup requirements are not
a guarantee that every study will be inexpensive.

## How to Use These Results

Synthetic research complements research with real people. It can help teams
explore questions and identify what to investigate next, but does not establish
actual demand, willingness to pay, adoption or retention. Evidence links make
interpretations inspectable; they do not guarantee that every model judgment is correct.
